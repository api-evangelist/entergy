The Mississippi Public Service Commission hosted a Winter Storm Preparedness Summit to review the January 2026 storm response and discuss ways to strengthen grid reliability. Entergy Mississippi leaders outlined restoration efforts, infrastructure impacts, and ongoing investments to enhance resilience and support future emergency coordination.
