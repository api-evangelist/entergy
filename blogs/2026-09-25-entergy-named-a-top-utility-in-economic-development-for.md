For the 19th consecutive year, Site Selection magazine has named Entergy a Top Utility in economic development. The communities we serve continue to attract some of the nation’s most competitive and power‑intensive industries in the United States. In 2025, Entergy Texas helped secure more than $30,000 in capital investments and create more than 700 new jobs within Southeast Texas.
