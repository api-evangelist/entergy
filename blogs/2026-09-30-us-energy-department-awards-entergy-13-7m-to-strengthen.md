The U.S. Energy Department has selected Entergy to receive $13.7 million in federal funding to improve the power delivery to Entergy’s customers and communities and support economic growth across Arkansas, Louisiana and Mississippi. The grid improvements will strengthen reliability, boost resilience and lower costs for customers.
