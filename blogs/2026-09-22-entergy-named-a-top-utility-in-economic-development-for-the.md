For the 19th consecutive year, Site Selection magazine has named Entergy a Top Utility in economic development. The communities we serve in Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi and Texas continue to attract some of the nation’s most competitive and power‑intensive industries. In 2025, Entergy helped secure more than $83 million in capital investments and create approximately 7,560 new direct jobs.
