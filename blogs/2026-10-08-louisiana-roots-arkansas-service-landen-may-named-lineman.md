Landen May never expected a college side job to lead him to a career in linework — much less to the field at a New Orleans Saints game. But on October 11, the Louisiana native and Entergy Arkansas lineman will return to his home state to be recognized as Entergy Arkansas’s Lineman of the Game during the Saints’ matchup against the Minnesota Vikings.
